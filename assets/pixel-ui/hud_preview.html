Blockbound HUD

The health, mana and boss bars, drawn from the pixel kit at the game's real positions on a 1080p screen. Use the buttons under it to take hits, heal, cast, get a cast refused, and fight the boss.

Text is Silkscreen, the font your game already uses. The fills are one white sprite tinted per bar, so health turning red when low and mana dimming are just colour changes.
